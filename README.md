# UnitedStewards

## Overview

UnitedStewards is an early-stage exploration of community-led financial
stewardship and shared-ledger tools. The project considers whether transparent,
consent-based records can improve financial management, assistance, and
collaboration while respecting participants' control of their information.

Sharealedger is a possible technology and services layer for this work. The
underlying ideas build on *Shared Ledgers, Trust, Fast Offerings, and Social
Commerce*, presented at the 2024 BYU Accounting Research Symposium.

## Concepts under exploration

These are discussion concepts, not adopted organizational decisions:

- **Member governance:** One model discussed is an LLC whose members elect or
  sustain a bishop as its presiding authority, with a COO responsible for
  operations and accountable to the members, and a treasurer responsible for
  financial administration. The legal authority and duties of each role would
  need to be defined in governing documents and reviewed by counsel.
- **Independent religious or business entity:** A separate, member-governed
  entity has also been considered, including whether a structure under
  Internal Revenue Code section 501(d) could be appropriate. In that concept,
  the entity could oversee member stewardship or business activities while
  Sharealedger provides technology or administrative services. Eligibility,
  governance, and tax treatment have not been established.
- **Shared-ledger tools:** A system could help participants and authorized
  stewards maintain accurate records, see agreed-upon transactions, and
  support financial learning or small-business activity. Possible use cases
  include straightforward financial-assistance workflows for community
  stewards and recordkeeping for small businesses. These are exploratory
  examples, not approved features or commitments.

The LLC governance model and the 501(d) concept are separate alternatives; no
entity form or relationship between them has been selected.

## Questions to resolve

- Which entity form and jurisdiction, if any, fit the intended purpose?
- What are the legal and tax consequences of member contributions,
  distributions, business activity, and any investment activity?
- What duties and decision-making powers belong to members, spiritual leaders,
  officers, and financial administrators?
- How should participant consent, privacy, account ownership, record access,
  and separation of funds be protected?
- What banking, charitable-solicitation, securities, licensing, or other
  regulatory requirements could apply?
- What relationship, if any, would the organization have with a church or
  other institution?

These questions require advice from qualified legal and tax professionals.
References to religious leadership or scripture describe ideas being explored;
they do not establish civil authority, institutional approval, or legal
validity.

## Status and scope

UnitedStewards is a concept under exploration. This repository does not
represent a formed or tax-exempt entity, a legal or tax opinion, or endorsement
by any church or other institution.

Personal journals, private correspondence, donor information, and identifying
details about individuals are outside the scope of this project repository and
should not be added to public materials.
